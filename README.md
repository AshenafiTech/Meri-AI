# Meri AI

![Meri AI Screenshot](client/public/meri-ai-screenshot.png)

Meri AI is an intelligent career assistant designed to help job seekers and professionals upskill, analyze their CVs, and connect with relevant communities. The platform supports multiple languages, including Ethiopian languages, making it accessible to a wider audience.

## How Meri AI Solves Real Problems

- **Skill Gap Analysis:** Users can upload their CVs and receive instant feedback on missing skills for their desired job roles. This helps them understand exactly what they need to learn to become competitive in the job market.
- **Personalized Learning Recommendations:** Based on the user's career goals and skill gaps, Meri AI recommends tailored learning resources and courses, saving users time and effort in finding the right materials.
- **Community Connection:** The platform suggests nearby professional communities and events, enabling users to network, find mentors, and stay updated with industry trends.
- **Multilingual Support:** By supporting Amharic, Afaan Oromo, Tigrigna, and more, Meri AI breaks language barriers and empowers users from diverse backgrounds to access career guidance.
- **Modern, User-Friendly Interface:** The clean UI and chat-based interaction make career planning and skill development engaging and easy to use.

## Why Meri AI?

Traditional career platforms often provide generic advice and lack local context. Meri AI leverages AI to deliver personalized, actionable feedback and connects users to real opportunities in their region and field. Whether you're a recent graduate or an experienced professional, Meri AI helps you take the next step in your career journey.
